# AVENGERS ARCHIVE — POWER STATS RUBRIC

**Classification:** Fan-Made Archive Assessment (Non-Official)  
**Scale:** 0 to 100 per attribute  
**Labeling Requirement:** Every UI radar chart must clearly state: `"Archive rating (fan-made)"`. No official Marvel claims.

## 1. Attributes & Tier Benchmarks

### 1. Strength (Physical raw power & lifting capacity)
- **0–20:** Average civilian human.
- **21–40:** Peak human athletic training (Hawkeye, Black Widow).
- **41–65:** Enhanced superhuman / Vibranium-augmented (Captain America, Winter Soldier, Black Panther).
- **66–85:** High-tier powered armor or sorcery-enhanced physique (Iron Man Hulkbuster/Mark 85, War Machine).
- **86–100:** God-tier / Planetary physical titans (Hulk, Thor, Thanos).

### 2. Speed (Reaction time, combat speed, travel velocity)
- **0–20:** Normal human mobility.
- **21–40:** Peak human reflex and agility (Hawkeye, Black Widow).
- **41–60:** Enhanced sprint and acrobatic maneuvers (Captain America, Black Panther).
- **61–80:** Supersonic flight / High-velocity thrusters (Iron Man, Thor with Mjolnir/Stormbreaker).
- **81–100:** Speedsters / Relativistic movement (Quicksilver, Captain Marvel binary flight).

### 3. Intellect (Strategic acuity, scientific genius, engineering mastery)
- **0–20:** Baseline intelligence.
- **21–40:** Practical tactical intuition.
- **41–65:** Master field commander / Spymaster (Captain America, Black Widow, Nick Fury).
- **66–85:** Advanced tactical genius / Sorcery scholars (Doctor Strange, Shuri, Loki).
- **86–100:** Multi-disciplinary visionary polymaths (Tony Stark, Bruce Banner, Hank Pym).

### 4. Durability (Resistance to trauma, blunt force, energy blasts, regeneration)
- **0–20:** Normal human vulnerability to small arms.
- **21–40:** High pain tolerance with protective Kevlar/tactical weave (Hawkeye, Black Widow).
- **41–65:** Enhanced cellular density / Vibranium weave (Captain America, Black Panther suit).
- **66–85:** Nanotech plating / Asgardian physiology (Iron Man Mark 50/85, Thor baseline).
- **86–100:** Nearly indestructible / Extreme regenerative healing (Hulk, awakened Thor).

### 5. Energy (Projection, manipulation, elemental and cosmic channeling)
- **0–20:** No intrinsic energy manipulation (pure physical fighters).
- **21–40:** External gadgetry / micro-explosives (Hawkeye trick arrows, Black Widow stingers).
- **41–65:** Channeled external kinetic energy (Black Panther kinetic pulse).
- **66–85:** Advanced energy repulsion / Arc Reactor beams / Lightning control (Iron Man, Thor).
- **86–100:** Reality-warping / Cosmic & Infinity energy channeling (Scarlet Witch, Captain Marvel).

### 6. Combat (Hand-to-hand mastery, martial arts, weapon proficiency, battlefield tactics)
- **0–20:** Untrained street brawler.
- **21–40:** Basic military hand-to-hand training.
- **41–65:** Highly accomplished martial artist or weapons specialist (Iron Man with AI assistance).
- **66–85:** Master martial artist across all Earth styles (Black Widow, Hawkeye, Black Panther).
- **86–100:** Unrivaled living weapon / Century-tested battlefield tactician (Captain America, Thor).
